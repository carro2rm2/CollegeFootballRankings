How this version ranks teams

Division hierarchy: Power-conference FBS teams and Notre Dame rank ahead of other FBS, followed by FCS, Division II and Division III. A lower-division team can jump a higher-division opponent only if it has a winning head-to-head record against that specific team.

Neutral-field strength: Road wins receive a 3-point résumé advantage over equivalent home wins. Home losses receive an additional 3-point penalty, while road losses receive 3 points of relief. Neutral-site games receive no venue adjustment. The Elo model also accounts for a 55-point home-field advantage.

Head-to-head: Direct results matter most when teams have comparable records. The bonus considers the venue-adjusted margin, so a road win is stronger evidence of neutral-field superiority than the same win at home. Circular results receive smaller adjustments.

Recent form: Recent games count more than early-season games. Winning streaks help, but the bonus is capped and cannot erase the full-season record.

Résumé and consistency: Quality wins help, but losses to below-average opponents are penalized more heavily. Blowout losses and shutouts carry additional penalties. Win-margin bonuses are deliberately small.

Opponent quality is estimated from an iterative schedule rating. Cross-division comparisons remain less certain when teams have few or no common opponents. This is an independent model, not an official poll.
