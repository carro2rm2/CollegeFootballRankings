How the model works

Each completed game updates both teams' Elo ratings based only on the winner and loser. The score and margin of victory are deliberately ignored: a one-point win and a 50-point win count the same.

Starting ratings are transparent structural assumptions: FBS power-conference teams start at 1800, other FBS teams at 1600, FCS at 1300, and Division II/III at 900. These are priors—not historical poll rankings—and game results move ratings from there. Home-field advantage is 55 rating points; the Elo update factor is 32.

Opponent strength propagates through the schedule as teams' ratings change. Rankings depend on the completeness and classification of the source data, and should not be treated as an official poll. D-II and D-III are currently assigned the same starting rating.

Data source: ESPN public college-football scoreboard feed. This page is an independent, non-commercial project.
