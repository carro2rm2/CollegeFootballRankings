How the rankings work

Final score: The ranking combines résumé score, recent form, head-to-head and opponent-adjusted Elo strength. Elo is explicitly included in the final power rating; it is not merely calculated and discarded.

Game strength: Elo is updated chronologically using opponent rating, a 55-point home-field advantage and a modest margin-of-victory adjustment. The final ranking includes 55% of each team's Elo movement from its division starting point.

Résumé: Quality wins are rewarded. Road wins receive a 3-point venue edge over equivalent home wins; home losses receive an additional penalty and road losses receive relief. Blowout losses and shutouts are penalized.

Head-to-head: Direct results between teams with similar records can shift their order. Winning on the road is treated as stronger evidence than the same margin at home. Cycles are dampened to avoid circular-result inflation.

Recent form: The six most recent games count more heavily, with a capped winning-streak bonus.

Division hierarchy: Power-conference FBS and Notre Dame are listed ahead of other FBS, followed by FCS, Division II and Division III. A lower-tier team can move ahead of an adjacent higher-tier opponent when it has a winning head-to-head record against that team.

Data source: CollegeFootballData API through a Cloudflare Worker. Independent project.
