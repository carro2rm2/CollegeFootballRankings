How the résumé model works

1. Quality wins directly drive the ranking. Every win starts at +22 résumé points, then earns up to 32 additional points for beating a highly rated opponent. Signature wins are also counted.

2. Consistency still matters. Every win earns a base reward and every loss carries a base penalty. A loss to a strong team is less damaging than a loss to a weak team; a bad loss can erase the value of several ordinary wins.

3. Margin is a supporting factor. Winning margin adds at most five points to a win or a small additional penalty to a loss, so running up the score cannot overwhelm the résumé.

4. Opponent quality is estimated with an Elo schedule model. The final table is sorted by résumé score, not by the intermediate Elo rating. Starting division offsets are small and are used only to help estimate opponent strength.

Ratings begin close together across divisions so division labels do not predetermine the rankings. Cross-division comparisons remain less certain when teams have few or no common opponents. This is an independent model, not an official poll.
